# Pet Boarding MVP

## A. Functional Requirements

### 1\. Search Pet Hotels by Location

Users can search for pet hotels in a specific area (e.g., Bangsar).

### 2\. View Available Dates and Times

Users can check the availability of pet hotels for their desired dates.

### 3\. View Pet Hotel Profiles

Each pet hotel has a profile page including:

* Description  
* Pricing  
* Photos  
* Facilities (e.g., grooming, play areas, veterinary care)  
* Policies (e.g., cancellation, check-in/out times)

### 4\. Book a Pet Hotel (Manual Process)

* Users can submit a booking request.  
* The booking is marked as “pending” until manually confirmed by your team.  
* Users receive a confirmation via email or in-app notification once processed.

### 5\. User and Pet Profile Management

Users can maintain their contact details and preferred locations.

Users can create and manage basic pet profiles (e.g., name, breed, special needs).

### 6.View Ratings and Reviews

Users can see ratings and reviews for each pet hotel to make informed decisions.

### 7\. Filter and Sort Search Results

Users can filter pet hotels by basic criteria (e.g., price, pet type).

Users can sort results (e.g., by rating or distance).

### 8\. Leave Feedback

Users can provide feedback on both the app and their pet hotel experience.

### 9\. Basic User Authentication

Users can sign up and log in using email (simple authentication).

### 10\. Booking Confirmation

Users receive a confirmation (manual, via email or in-app) after their booking is processed.

## B. Non-Functional Requirement

* Prioritize Security and Basic Usability: Invest enough effort to make the app feel safe and straightforward to use. These are dealbreakers for users.  
* Keep Performance Simple: Aim for “functional,” not “flawless.” Avoid over-engineering at this stage.  
* Defer Scalability: Don’t waste time on this until you know the app has traction.
