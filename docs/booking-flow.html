Submit Booking Request

Tap Submit Request. Your booking is created instantly with a Pending status — it is not yet confirmed.

STEP 7

Notification Emails Sent

The system automatically emails you a booking receipt and notifies the hotel admin of the new request. No human action needed at this step.

Booking receipt → you New request alert → admin In-app notification

STEP 8

Waiting for Review

Your booking is in the queue. You can check its status any time under My Bookings. The hotel admin will review and act on your request manually.

My Bookings page Status: Pending

🛎️
STEP 9

Admin Reviews the Request

The hotel admin opens the admin panel, views the booking details (pet info, dates, notes), and manually decides to Confirm or Cancel.

Admin panel Booking list Filter by Pending
Admin Decision

✅ Confirmed

Booking status changes to Confirmed. You receive a confirmation email and an in-app notification. Your stay is locked in!

❌ Declined / Cancelled

Booking status changes to Cancelled. You receive a cancellation email explaining the outcome. You can search for another hotel.
