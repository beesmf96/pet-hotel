# Personas and their seed data

## Asked
"Can we have few personas, eg. a dog owner, that planning to have vacation at
1-5 nov". Dogs and cats only; the user chose a doc plus seed data.

## Done
- `docs-src/personas.md`: Aisyah (dog owner going on holiday), Daniel (dog and
  cat, books late), Mei Ling (careful first-timer with a cat) and Encik Rahman
  (owner, capacity 6). Each has what they expect, a journey and the features it
  tests. A closing section lists two clarity gaps the stories exposed: "Book
  Now" sends a request, and "the hotel will confirm shortly" while a request
  can stay pending until after check-in.
- `PersonaSeeder`, called last in `DatabaseSeeder`: the four accounts and their
  pets; Rahman's Paws House (6 spots, four guests confirmed for 1–5 Nov, so two
  left each night) and Whiskers & Wags (closed on 3 Nov); Daniel's pending
  request for Oyen on 1–3 Nov and a completed stay for Bruno six weeks ago.
  Dates move to the next November once this year's has passed.
- It runs after `HotelAvailabilitySeeder` on purpose: 1 November 2026 is a
  Sunday, and the demo closes every hotel on Sundays.
- `local-setup.md` lists the persona logins.
- Backlog: "Say plainly that a booking is a request" and "An answer deadline
  for requests", from the two gaps; "Show success messages on every page",
  from the journey run below.

## Not done
- The two clarity gaps are not fixed; both are now backlog entries.
- Dev database not reseeded; it still holds the e2e data from 2026-10-11.

  pop-up, confirm until full, the refused confirm, cancel. 14 steps passed.
  It found the missing success message after a review.
