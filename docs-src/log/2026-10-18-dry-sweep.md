# First DRY sweep

## Asked
`/dry-sweep` over the whole codebase. The user approved every Extract
finding and chose "keep" for every Ask finding.

## Done
- Tests first: pinned the stay lines in the customer emails, the date and
  status-badge colour in both booking lists, the created-at dates in the
  admin users, reviews, owners and hotels tables, and nights on the booking
  form, detail and My Bookings pages.
- Backend:
  - `App\Support\Dates` holds the user-facing formats (`DAY`, `DAY_TIME`, `SHORT`).
  - `Booking::checkInText()`, `checkOutText()`, `nights()` and
    `nightsText()` replace the copies in `BookingRequested`, `BookingConfirmed`,
    `OwnerBookingNotification` and the owner booking pop-up.
  - `App\Enums\BookingStatus` gives both panels their status labels and
    badge colours.
  - Panel tables get `d M Y` as the default date format, set once in
    `AppServiceProvider`.
- Frontend:
  - `nightsBetween()` and `nightsLabel()` in `stay.js` replace three copies
    of the nights math and four copies of the "night(s)" text.
  - The `link-bold` utility in `app.css` replaces the bold text-link class bundle.
- `dry-keep:` markers, with reasons:
  - `StatusPill.vue` labels: JS twin of `BookingStatus`.
  - The `'j M Y'` dates in the pet-clash error: shorter, inside a sentence.
  - "Pets per night", at each of its 5 fields: a label, not a constant.
  - The cancelled email: plain dates, no hotel times.
  - "(1 nights)" on My Bookings: kept as it was.
  - The owner panel's teal hex: PHP cannot read `tokens.css`.

## Not done
- The admin hotels table still shows `created_at` month first (`Oct 25, 2030`).
  It used Filament's own default, so it got an explicit `'M j, Y'` to keep
  output the same. Every other panel table is day first.
- "(1 nights)" on My Bookings stays, by choice.
- Seen but not repeated code, so left alone: the plain `<a href="/bookings">`
  in `BookingDetailPage.vue`, and the progress bar hex in `app.js`.
